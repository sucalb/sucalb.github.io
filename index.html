ABOUT ME

Good design is quiet. It just works.

I'm Lucas Bui, a web designer from Vietnam. Much of my work starts in the classroom: I build tools for an English center and its teaching assistants, and interactive labs that help teachers explain science, math and geography.

I care about interfaces that feel obvious the first time you use them — and about shipping them, not just drawing them.
